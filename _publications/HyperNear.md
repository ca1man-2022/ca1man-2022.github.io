In this work, we explore adversarial attacks on hypergraph-based models, revealing their structural vulnerabilities. We introduce HyperNear, the first node injection attack framework for HNNs, leveraging homophily constraints for stealth. Experiments demonstrate its high attack efficacy, transferability, and unnoticeability. Our analysis of unnoticeability metrics deepens the understanding of hypergraph structure and model robustness, paving the way for future work on strengthening hypergraph-based models.
