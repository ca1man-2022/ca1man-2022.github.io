This survey organizes graph out-of-distribution (GOOD) detection methods into six categories based on how they distinguish out-of-distribution inputs. It reviews representative datasets, distribution shifts, benchmarks, and evaluation metrics. Controlled synthetic comparisons show how representative methods respond to different shifts and graph conditions, and the survey outlines open challenges and future directions.
